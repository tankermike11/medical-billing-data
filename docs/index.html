Turning public billing data into patient advocacy intelligence

This pipeline ingests 15 public medical billing data sources — diagnosis codes, procedure codes, drug codes, claim denial codes, and marketplace plan documents — into a unified database that powers real-time advocacy for patients navigating the healthcare billing system.

15 Data Sources ~2M+ Records ICD · HCPCS · NDC · DRG · CARC · RARC ACA Marketplace Plans SBC PDF Extraction
🔍

Plain-English Code Lookup

Translate any code from an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) into a clear, human-readable description — instantly.

  • ICD-10 diagnosis & procedure codes
  • HCPCS / CPT procedure codes
  • Place of Service codes
  • MS-DRG hospital groupings
❌

Claim Denial Intelligence

Identify exactly why a claim was denied or adjusted by decoding CARC and RARC codes — the standardized reason codes payers attach to every remittance.

  • 308 Claim Adjustment Reason Codes
  • 1,198 Remittance Advice Remark Codes
  • Actionable denial context for appeals
💊

Drug & NDC Lookup

Cross-reference National Drug Codes on pharmacy and infusion claims to verify drug identity, dosage form, and labeler — critical for prescription billing disputes.

  • 113,516 FDA-registered drug products
  • Proprietary and generic name mapping
  • DEA schedule and route of administration
📋

Plan Cost-Sharing Verification

Look up a patient's actual plan cost-sharing structure — deductibles, copays, out-of-pocket maximums — extracted directly from the legally-binding Summary of Benefits and Coverage documents.

  • 4,500+ individual market medical plans
  • 699 stand-alone dental plans
  • In-network deductible, OOP max, copays
🏥

DRG Validation

Verify inpatient hospital DRG assignments and understand the grouper logic — a single wrong DRG can shift payment by thousands of dollars.

  • 771 MS-DRG V42 definitions
  • MDC classification and surgical/medical type
  • ICD-10-PCS procedure code cross-reference
⚡

Automated Data Refresh

Every source is versioned, hash-checked, and logged. Re-running the pipeline only processes data that has actually changed — keeping the database current with minimal overhead.

  • Weekly: FDA NDC drug database
  • Quarterly: HCPCS, CARC, RARC, POS
  • Annual: ICD codes, marketplace plans
