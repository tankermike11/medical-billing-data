Partnerships in the works:
- We are working on a partnership with wellthy where they would handle referrals for escalations where members need or want a human case manager or reviewer.
- We have a partnership with MyID. Lifetime members get this bracelet for free and this company is offering us the ability to access QR scan data by our members.
- We are working on a partnership with Uber Health to support ambulatory NEMT in non-rural areas. We need other vendors to provide holistic capacity across locations and medical needs types.
- Prior to us using Uber Health as one of our vendors to help us coordinate NEMT transport across the country, we will be providing Group customers (on the Platinum and Emergent Premier plans) with limited uber credits. The intent is that each year, members will log into the app to "enroll" for these credits much like they would for Amex Platinum benefits. Platinum members (highest tier) would receive $100 in credits per year and Emergent Premier (next highest tier) would receive $50 in credits per year. These would not be gated like other benefits that require you to be an in-patient or have an emergency event. This will simply be geofenced to medical locations like nursing homes, doctor's offices, PT centers, etc. Members will also be able to coordinate / schedule rides for family members. The intent is that it will drive workplace attendance (they won't have to leave to drive a parent to the doctor), it will increase in-app engagement, and it will be a low cost benefit to utilize which will support broker conversion.
- We are considering partnering with GoodBill or other similar vendor to take our referral for bill error identification / denial appeal / bill negotiation human case review and management.
 
Background on app status
- Our app has significant issues. The auth is different between our app and member portal (website) so you need to set up 2 different accounts. In addition, at least for the last 6-12 months, when you attempt to create an account in the app, the confirmation code is sent to your email, and when you return to the app to enter it, the app refreshes back to the home screen. This leads to a situation where you cannot fully create an account and/or login. Lastly, you cannot update or add a credit card / payment method (to support annual or monthly members), you cannot start or view claims, and the plan experience is basic (just a PDF file that you download). Because of this, our apple Appstore rating is 1.6.
- We are planning to relaunch the app with updated plan experience, ability to view/track claims, ability to add/edit/delete payment methods, ability to view payment history, and a fixed authentication (and unified) experience. This will be done in the next 30 days. In the following 30 days, we will then add the ability to start a claim (basic submission; not SAM or guided flow), new trip submission flow (because for some plans, you need to provide in order to have coverage), and native wallet (apple, android; to support geofencing) for member ID.
